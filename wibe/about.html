About WIBE

We believe everyone deserves to hear music the way it was meant to be heard.

Our Story

Born from a Love of Sound

WIBE was founded in 2019 by a group of audio engineers and music lovers frustrated with the compromise between sound quality, comfort, and design.

We set out to build headphones that professionals respect and everyday listeners love. Four years later, our products are used by Grammy-winning producers, Olympic athletes, and millions of music fans worldwide.

Every WIBE product starts with a simple question: "Would we use this every day?" If the answer isn't an immediate yes, we go back to the drawing board.
