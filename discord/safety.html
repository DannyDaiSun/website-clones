Safety Center

Discord is committed to creating a safe and positive experience for everyone. Here's how we protect our community.

Our Safety Approach

🛡️

Safety by Design

We build safety into every feature from the start, not as an afterthought.

👁️

Transparency

We publish regular transparency reports detailing our enforcement actions.

🤝

Partnership

We work with safety organizations, researchers, and our community.

🔧

User Tools

We give users the controls they need to customize their experience.

Moderation Tools

AutoMod automatically detects and blocks harmful content before it's ever posted. Set up keyword filters, spam protection, and mention limits for your server.

  • Custom keyword filters
  • Spam and mention protection
  • Regex pattern matching
  • Configurable actions (block, alert, timeout)

Set verification requirements for new members before they can send messages. Choose from None, Low, Medium, High, or Highest.

  • Email verification
  • Account age requirements
  • Server membership duration
  • Phone verification (Highest)

Discord scans media content and blocks explicit material. Customize the level of scanning for your server members and DMs.

  • Explicit image detection
  • Scan all media or just from new members
  • Configurable per server
  • DM safe messaging options

Users can report content or behavior that violates our Community Guidelines. Our Trust & Safety team reviews all reports.

  • In-app reporting with categories
  • Message and user reports
  • Server reports
  • 24/7 Trust & Safety team

Control who can DM you, add you as a friend, and what information is visible on your profile.

  • DM restrictions per server
  • Friend request controls
  • Invisible mode
  • Block and mute users

Tips for Parents & Educators

1

Set Up Together

Help your teen set up their account. Review privacy settings and enable Safe Direct Messaging.

2

Know the Features

Understand how servers, channels, and DMs work. Try using Discord yourself to learn the platform.

3

Enable Protections

Turn on explicit content filtering, restrict who can DM your teen, and review joined servers.

4

Have Conversations

Talk about online safety, who they talk to, and what to do if they encounter something uncomfortable.
