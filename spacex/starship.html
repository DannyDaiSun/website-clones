STARSHIP

THE WORLD'S MOST POWERFUL LAUNCH VEHICLE

Starship is designed to carry both crew and cargo to Earth orbit, the Moon, Mars, and beyond. It is the largest and most powerful rocket ever flown.

Two-Stage System

Starship is a fully reusable transportation system consisting of two stages: the Starship spacecraft (upper stage) and the Super Heavy booster (first stage).

121 meters Total height
9 meters Diameter
5,000 tons Liftoff mass

Both stages are designed to be fully and rapidly reusable — a breakthrough that dramatically reduces the cost of access to space.
