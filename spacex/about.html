About SpaceX

SpaceX designs, manufactures, and launches advanced rockets and spacecraft.

Our Mission

SpaceX was founded in 2002 by Elon Musk with the goal of reducing space transportation costs and enabling the colonization of Mars.

The company has developed the Falcon 1, Falcon 9, Falcon Heavy, and Starship launch vehicles, as well as the Dragon spacecraft. SpaceX is the first private company to send a spacecraft to the International Space Station, and the first to achieve orbital rocket reusability.

Starlink

Starlink is the world's largest satellite constellation, delivering high-speed, low-latency broadband internet to locations where connectivity has been unreliable or completely unavailable.

With over 6,000 satellites in orbit, Starlink provides coverage in 70+ countries and serves over 4 million subscribers globally.
