📝 Submission Guidelines

What to Submit

On-Topic: Anything that good hackers would find interesting. That includes more than hacking and startups. If you had to reduce it to a sentence, the answer might be: anything that gratifies one's intellectual curiosity.

Off-Topic

Most stories about politics, or crime, or sports. Videos of pratfalls or disasters. If they'd cover it on TV news, it's probably off-topic.

Title

Please use the original title, unless it is misleading or linkbait; don't editorialize.

Duplicates

Please don't submit a story that's already on the front page, or was there recently. Search before posting.
